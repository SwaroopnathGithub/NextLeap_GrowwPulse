What's actually being said

Fees & charges (382 mentions, 60% of the specific-fee-term subset are 1-2★)

"Brokerage fees are too high, Dp charge 24 rupees... when I calculate my monthly brokerage charge I am fully shocked" — 1★
"This application has the highest brokerage charges compared to other applications." — 1★
"Here Brokerage charge is very very high. This will finish your hard earned profit." — 1★

Several reviewers explicitly compare to Zerodha as cheaper. A recurring secondary complaint: a flat ₹5/trade charge that surprises beginners who expected free trading.

→ See the companion Fee Explainer for the detail and a recommended user-facing fix.

Order execution / trading (379 mentions)

Complaints cluster around slow order fills, FNO glitches, and confusion around stop-loss/limit order behavior.

Customer support (337 mentions)

Mixed — several reviewers praise fast, clear responses, but a recurring complaint thread is "no reply" or slow resolution on fund/withdrawal issues specifically.

App performance (297 mentions)

Crashes and lag reported inconsistently, not concentrated in a single app version in this sample — worth a follow-up cut by appVersion if engineering wants to investigate further.

Withdrawal/payout (88 mentions, low volume but high severity)

A handful of reviews describe funds stuck for multiple days with unresolved support tickets — small in volume but reputationally costly (these tend to be long, detailed 1★ reviews).

Recommendations

  1. Prioritize fee transparency — brokerage/DP charges are the single most consistent complaint category across all 12 weeks. A pre-trade cost preview or a simple "what you'll pay" explainer is the highest-leverage fix available from this data alone.
  2. Audit the withdrawal/support loop — low volume, but these are the reviews most likely to show up in press or social screenshots. Worth a support-ticket SLA check even though it's only 0.7% of the sample.
  3. No action needed on UI/UX — it's the top-mentioned theme but mostly positive; don't let mention volume alone trigger a redesign.

Methodology note
