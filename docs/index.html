How this was built

Reviews were scraped from the Google Play Store listing for Groww (com.nextbillion.groww), then analyzed for rating trends and recurring themes. The findings feed an n8n workflow with a human approval gate: an LLM (Groq) drafts the pulse and fee-explainer text, a person approves it, and only then does it get appended to a tracking doc and queued as a Gmail draft — never sent automatically. See the full workflow breakdown for details.

This is a product-review synthesis exercise, not Groww's official fee schedule or policy. Exact rates should be confirmed against Groww's own published charges before being used in any user-facing communication.
